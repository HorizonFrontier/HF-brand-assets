# HFR Brand Standards v1.0

## 1. Color Palette (Strict)
**Core Colors**
*   Paper White: `#FAFAFA`
*   HFR Charcoal: `#1F2421` (Primary Text)
*   HFR Teal: `#239C8D` (Primary Brand)
*   HFR Black: `#0E110C` (Backgrounds)

**Teal Scale**
*   Dark: `#1C7F73`
*   Soft: `#5FBFB2`
*   Tint: `#D9F1EE`

**Functional**
*   Success: `#2E7D32`
*   Warning: `#B26A00`
*   Error: `#9B1C1C`
*   Info: `#2F5D8A`

## 2. Typography
**Primary Typeface:** Arial, Helvetica, or System Sans-Serif.
*   **Headings:** Bold, Charcoal (#1F2421)
*   **Body:** Regular, Charcoal (#1F2421) or Slate Gray (#4A4F4C)
*   **Legal/Metadata:** Ash Gray (#B8BCBA)

## 3. Tone of Voice
*   Professional, neutral, and structured.
*   No "fluff" or marketing exaggeration.
*   Engineering-led and ISO-compliant.
